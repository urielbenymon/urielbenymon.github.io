Research Experience

  • Bancroft Research Scholar | Columbia University (February 2026 – April 2026). Advisor: Professor Ansaf Salleb-Aouissi.
    • Developed a hypertension-prediction study using NHANES 2017–March 2020 pre-pandemic data, conducting exploratory data analysis and building a preliminary multi-model ML pipeline.
    • Independently extended the project into a leakage-controlled, cross-validated study of 8,024 participants with five ML models, hyperparameter tuning, bootstrap inference, and SHAP-based interpretability.
  • RISE Scholar | Columbia University (April 2025 – July 2025). Advisor: Professor Paul Blaer.
    • Benchmarked PostgreSQL’s B-Tree index against other index types (Hash, BRIN, etc.) using a custom Java/SQL environment across randomized, standardized workloads.
    • Began testing lightweight B-Tree modifications (node capacity/configuration changes) under concurrent operations to explore performance trade-offs.

Independent Research Projects

  • Project Lead | System-Prompt Invariance for LLM-as-Judge Study (January 2026 – August 2026).
    • Led a five-person research team originating from a Columbia University LLM course project. Designed linguistic perturbation experiments and developed a Python evaluation pipeline comprising 324 judgments across three LLM graders, 108 model responses, and four response-generating models.
    • Recruited blinded volunteer human raters to validate evaluation outcomes, obtaining Krippendorff’s alpha of approximately 0.93 and 98% exact agreement with automated scores. Grader format compliance ranged from approximately 57% to 100%.
